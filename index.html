PLATE I · Travel e-commerce · product page · mobile

The not-yet buyer

Observation. People planning a trip more than a week out browse, compare and leave. They aren’t lost, just early.

Mechanism. Trade a discount for an email, then let CRM bring them back when the trip gets close.
